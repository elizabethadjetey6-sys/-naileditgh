Before you book

Booking Policy

Please read our policies carefully before securing your appointment.

Appointments

If you cannot find a suitable slot, please call or WhatsApp 0552518871 so we can check if another option is available.

If you need additional services such as toes, soak-off or pedicure, please inform us before your appointment so enough time can be allocated.

Payment & Confirmation

NO PAYMENT = NO CONFIRMED APPOINTMENT. Your appointment is only confirmed once the required payment has been made. Unpaid bookings may be cancelled and the slot released.

Online bookings are sent only after a successful Paystack payment. Please keep your Paystack payment reference for your records.

Late Arrivals

A 15-minute grace period is allowed. After 20 minutes, your appointment may be adjusted to fit the remaining time or cancelled. No refund will be given for cancellations caused by excessive lateness.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Cancellations or rescheduling must be made at least 10 hours before your appointment to avoid losing your payment. Missed appointments and late cancellations may result in the loss of your payment.

Soak-Off

If you have old nail enhancements that need to be removed, please book a soak-off or inform us beforehand. Soak-off is not free and requires additional time. If you require a soak-off, kindly arrive 30 minutes earlier.

Guests & Studio Etiquette

  • No children or pets are allowed.
  • A maximum of one guest is permitted per client.
  • Please use earphones/EarPods when watching videos or listening to music.
  • Keep phone volume low and take calls quietly.
  • We kindly ask everyone to help us maintain a calm studio environment.
